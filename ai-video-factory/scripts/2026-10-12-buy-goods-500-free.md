## Narration
Wairimu sells tea and mandazi in Nyeri. A cup here, two mandazi there. Most of her customers pay less than
five hundred shillings. She's heard that a Lipa na M-PESA till costs money, so she still uses her personal
number. Here's what changed. Since the seventh of August, businesses on Buy Goods pay no fee to receive
payments of up to five hundred shillings. Before, the free limit was two hundred. Above five hundred, the
charge is zero point five five percent, and it's capped at two hundred shillings. So for a kibanda like
Wairimu's, most sales now cost her nothing to receive. Getting a till is done online, on the M-PESA Business
Hub, at hub dot m pesa for business dot c o dot k e. A till also keeps business money apart from personal
money. Check the current tariff before you apply. How do your customers pay you? Tell me below.
