## Narration
The biggest YouTuber in the world just admitted something most creators never say out loud.
His charity videos perform worse. MrBeast spent ten million dollars building a village in Ghana,
and when a follower pointed out that humanitarian videos get less engagement, he didn't argue.
He said helping people brings him purpose, and even if no one watches, he'll keep making them.
Here's why that's a smart business lesson, not just a nice quote.
Number one: not every piece of content has to be your most viral.
Number two: purpose builds trust, and trust is what keeps an audience for years.
Number three: let your most popular work fund your most meaningful work.
So if you run a business, ask yourself: what would you keep doing even if no one clapped?
