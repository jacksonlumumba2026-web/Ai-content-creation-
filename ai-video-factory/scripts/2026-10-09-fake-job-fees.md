## Narration
Wanjiru has applied for jobs for months. Then an email arrives: an appointment letter, with the Kenya Revenue
Authority's name on it. She got the job. But there's one condition: pay four thousand five hundred shillings
as a processing and clearance fee. Her heart says pay. Her gut says wait. In September, KRA warned Kenyans
about exactly this kind of fake letter, and reminded job seekers that its recruitment is free. Other
employers, like the Kenya Ports Authority, have warned about fake recruiters too. So here's the rule. If a job
asks you to pay first, stop. Check the employer's official website and social media pages. And never send
money to secure a job. Wanjiru deletes the email, and keeps her money for the next real opportunity. Share this
with a job seeker you know.
