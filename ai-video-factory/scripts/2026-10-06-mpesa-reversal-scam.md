## Narration
Picture a shopkeeper in Nyeri. Let's call her Wairimu. Her phone rings. A man sounds panicked. "Sorry, I
sent you money by mistake. It's my school fees. Please send it back." Wairimu wants to help. But here's how
this scam works. First, money arrives, or sometimes just a fake message that looks like M-Pesa. Then comes
the call, urgent and emotional. If you send it back yourself, the scammer can also ask for the original to be
reversed, and you lose twice. The safe way is the official one. A real mistake gets reversed through
Safaricom, when the sender forwards the M-PESA message to four five six. So Wairimu says: please request a
reversal. The caller hangs up. If you think you've been targeted, call Safaricom on one hundred, or two three
four. Has someone tried this on you? Tell me below, and share this with a shop owner.
