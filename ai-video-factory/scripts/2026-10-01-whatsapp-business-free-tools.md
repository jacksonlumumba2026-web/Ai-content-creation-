## Narration
Picture a home baker in Kisumu. Let's call her Achieng. All day, customers message her. How much is a
cake? Do you deliver? Are you open? She types the same answers again and again, and at night, messages go
unanswered. Then she switches to the free WhatsApp Business app, and sets up four tools. Number one: a
catalog, so customers see her cakes, photos and prices right inside the chat. Number two: quick replies.
She saves her common answers, and sends them by typing a slash. Number three: greeting and away messages,
so new customers get an instant hello, and late-night customers know when she'll reply. Number four:
labels, to mark every chat as new, paid or delivered. Same phone. Same customers. Hours saved every week.
Which one will you set up today? Tell me below.
