## Narration
Chebet sells second-hand clothes in Eldoret, and December is her biggest month. Every week she puts some
money aside for December stock. And every week, something comes up. School shopping. A small loan to a
cousin. By November, the stock money is gone. So this year, she tries M-Shwari Lock Savings. Here's how it
works. She moves money from her M-Shwari account into a locked account, and picks how long it stays there.
While it's locked, it earns a higher interest rate than her regular M-Shwari savings. And on the date she
chose, the money comes back to her M-Shwari, with the interest. Two things to know. You can't touch it before
the date, that's the whole point. And interest rates change, so check the current rate first. This is not
financial advice. What would you lock away for your business? Tell me below.
