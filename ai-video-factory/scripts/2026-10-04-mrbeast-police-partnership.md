## Narration
When MrBeast wanted one hundred police officers to hunt him, he didn't hire actors. Instead, he partnered
with the Indiana State Police. Think about why that worked. The troopers took part off duty. The department
was paid for the use of its equipment. And if they caught all five players in twelve hours, they kept a
five hundred thousand dollar prize. They did, with about ninety minutes to spare. MrBeast got something
money alone can't buy: real officers, a real training site, and a story people believed. The police got
the prize, and millions of people watching their troopers win. Both sides came out ahead. So here's the
lesson for your business. Number one: find a partner who gains something real, not just a favor. Number
two: put clear rules and rewards in writing. Number three: make sure both names look good in the final
story. Who could your business partner with? Tell me below.
