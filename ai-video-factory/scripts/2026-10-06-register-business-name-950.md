## Narration
Picture a woman in Kisumu who makes beautiful handmade bags. Let's call her Faith. Her customers love them.
Then a shop in town says: we want to stock your bags. Just send us your business registration certificate.
Faith doesn't have one. She thinks it's expensive and complicated. Here's the good news. According to the
Business Registration Service, registering a business name costs nine hundred and fifty shillings, and you
can do it online. Step one: log in to eCitizen and open the Business Registration Service. Step two: choose
registration of a business name, fill in your details, then sign the form it creates and upload it again.
Step three: pay the nine hundred and fifty shillings when the prompt comes to your phone. Faith applies that
evening. Fees can change, so check eCitizen first. Is your business registered yet? Tell me below.
