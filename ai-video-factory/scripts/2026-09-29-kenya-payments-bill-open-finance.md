## Narration
Kenya just published a new payments bill, and it could change how your business gets paid and borrows.
On September twenty-second, the Central Bank of Kenya and the National Treasury released the draft
National Payment System Bill. Here's what it means. Number one: open finance. With your consent,
licensed third-party apps could access your bank and mobile money data. That could mean faster loans
and better tools built on your real sales history. Number two: payment providers would have to build
systems that share that data securely. Number three: tougher rules, including capital requirements of
up to two hundred and fifty million shillings for electronic money issuers. Nothing is law yet. You can
share your views until October ninth, and public forums are running in towns across Kenya.
Would you let an app see your M-Pesa history for a better loan? Tell me below.
