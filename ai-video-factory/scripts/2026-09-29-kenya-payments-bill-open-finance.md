## Narration
Picture a shop owner in Nakuru. Let's call her Wanjiku. Every day, hundreds of M-Pesa payments land on
her phone. But when she asks her bank for a loan to stock up for December, the answer is no. The bank
can't see her sales. Now, a new bill could change that. On September twenty-second, the Central Bank of
Kenya and the National Treasury released the draft National Payment System Bill. The big idea is open
finance. With Wanjiku's consent, licensed apps could access her bank and mobile money data, and see her
real sales history. Payment providers would have to share that data securely, and electronic money
issuers would face capital requirements of up to two hundred and fifty million shillings. Nothing is law
yet. Kenyans can share their views until October ninth. So, would you let an app see your M-Pesa history
for a better loan? Tell me below.
