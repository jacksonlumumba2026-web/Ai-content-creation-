## Narration
Picture a young founder in Mombasa. Let's call her Amina. Everyone tells her the same thing. If you want to
build a real startup in Kenya, you have to move to Nairobi. But this year's global startup rankings tell a
different story. In StartupBlink's 2026 index, Mombasa climbed twenty-two places. Nairobi slipped from one
hundred and seventh to one hundred and sixteenth. Kenya is still number one in Eastern Africa, with six
hundred and fifty-nine tracked startups. But the country fell three places globally, and growth was flat, at
minus zero point two percent. Here's the honest part. Nairobi is still more than thirty-six times bigger than
Mombasa by ecosystem score. So Amina doesn't have to move tomorrow. But the map is changing, and founder
activity is spreading to cities like Mombasa and Kisumu. Where would you build your business? Tell me below.
