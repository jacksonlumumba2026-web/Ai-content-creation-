## Narration
Baraka is a welder in Thika. A company wants him to make gates for their new building. Good money. Then the
company sends one message: please send your KRA PIN. Baraka doesn't have one. Someone in town says an agent
can do it for a fee. Here's the thing. You can apply yourself, online, on iTax, and there's no fee to apply.
Step one: go to i tax dot k r a dot g o dot k e. Step two: choose Individual, then Online Form. Step three:
fill in your details, your income source, and submit. KRA says residents get the PIN certificate after
completing the online form. The law asks for a KRA PIN for many things: registering a business name, opening
a bank account, business permits, and contracts with government. Baraka sends his PIN that evening, and gets
the gates job. Do you have your KRA PIN yet? Tell me below.
