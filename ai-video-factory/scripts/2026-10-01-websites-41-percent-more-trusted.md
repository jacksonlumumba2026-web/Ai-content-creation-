## Narration
Picture two bakeries on the same street. Same prices, same great bread. One only has an Instagram page.
The other has a simple website with its menu, location and reviews. A new customer needs a birthday cake.
Which one do they trust? A 2026 survey by web host DreamHost asked more than twelve hundred American
consumers how they decide which local businesses to trust. Businesses with a website were seen as
forty-one percent more trustworthy. After online reviews, a website was the strongest trust signal. And
among eighteen to twenty-four year olds, seventy-two percent said a website is necessary for credibility.
Here's the twist. People were comfortable spending about one hundred and seventy-seven dollars on a
business website, but only thirty-six dollars through an Instagram or TikTok shop. So the bakery with the
website doesn't just get trusted. It gets the bigger orders. Which bakery is your business? Follow for
more growth tips.
