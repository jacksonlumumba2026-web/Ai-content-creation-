## Narration
If your business only lives on Instagram or WhatsApp, you could be losing customers without knowing it.
A 2026 survey by web host DreamHost asked more than twelve hundred American consumers how they decide
which local businesses to trust. The result: businesses with a website are seen as forty-one percent more
trustworthy than those without. After online reviews, a website was the strongest trust signal.
Young people care even more. Seventy-two percent of eighteen to twenty-four year olds said a website is
necessary for credibility. And here's the money part. On average, people were comfortable spending about
one hundred and seventy-seven dollars on a business website, but only thirty-six dollars through an
Instagram or TikTok shop. So social media gets you noticed, but a website helps you get trusted and paid.
