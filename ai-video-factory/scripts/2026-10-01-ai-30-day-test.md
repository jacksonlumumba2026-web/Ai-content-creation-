## Narration
Eighty-two percent of small business employers have already invested in AI tools. That's from the Small
Business and Entrepreneurship Council's 2026 tech survey. But buying AI and getting results are two
different things. Here's a simple way to start: the thirty-day test. Step one: pick one task you repeat
every week, like replying to customer questions or writing product descriptions. Step two: give the AI
clear limits and your own approved information, like your price list and policies. Step three: a human
checks every result before it goes out. After thirty days, compare. How many hours did you save? Did the
quality stay the same? If it worked, keep it and pick the next task. If not, you lost very little.
One task. Thirty days. Real numbers. Which task would you test first? Tell me in the comments.
