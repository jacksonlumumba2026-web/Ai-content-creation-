## Narration
Picture a small clothing shop owner. Let's call her Grace. Every week she answers the same customer
questions again and again. Sizes, delivery, prices. Like eighty-two percent of small business employers in
the SBE Council's 2026 survey, she has already paid for an AI tool. But it just sits there. So she tries a
thirty-day test. Step one: one task only, answering those customer questions. Step two: she gives the AI
clear limits and her own price list and policies. Step three: she checks every reply before it goes out.
At the end of the month, she compares. Did it save her time? Did the answers stay accurate? If yes, she
keeps it and picks the next task. If not, she lost very little. One task. Thirty days. Real numbers.
What task would you give AI first? Tell me in the comments.
