## Narration
Imagine living inside a grocery store, and the last person to leave wins. That's how MrBeast's newest video
started. Day sixty-seven. Four players are still there. Then he changes the rules. New deal: work as a team,
eat everything in the grocery store, and win one million dollars, even if it takes up to a year. And he didn't
just walk away. He promised to restock the store, and add beds, showers, a nutritionist, a personal trainer
and a gym. So the video doesn't really end. It turns into a story millions of people will want to follow.
Here's the business lesson. Number one: end with a reason for people to come back. Number two: make your
people part of the story, not just your product. Number three: look after them while you do it. Would you take
that deal? Tell me below.
