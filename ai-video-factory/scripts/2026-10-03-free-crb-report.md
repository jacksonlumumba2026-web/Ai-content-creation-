## Narration
Picture a salon owner in Thika. Let's call her Wanjiru. She applies for a loan to buy new chairs, and it's
rejected. No clear reason. She feels stuck. Here's what most people don't know. In Kenya, you're entitled
to one free credit report every year from each licensed credit reference bureau, like TransUnion, Metropol
or Creditinfo. Here's how. Go to the bureau's website, app or USSD code, enter your ID number, and request
your free report. It can take a day or two to arrive. Wanjiru checks, and finds an old mobile loan she
forgot to clear. If something is wrong on your report, you can dispute it with the bureau in writing. If
it's right, pay it off and ask for your status to be updated. So before you apply for any loan, check your
report first. Have you checked yours? Tell me below. This is not financial advice.
