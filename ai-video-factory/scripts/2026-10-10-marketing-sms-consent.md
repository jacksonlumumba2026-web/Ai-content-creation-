## Narration
Mwangi has a list of almost every customer who ever called his salon in Thika. Hundreds of numbers. This
weekend he's running a discount, so he types one promo message for all of them. He's about to hit send. But
here's what he doesn't know. In Kenya, sending marketing messages without consent can break the Data
Protection Act. The Data Protection Commissioner ordered one lender, Platinum Credit, to pay four hundred
thousand shillings to someone it kept sending promotions to. And Pepinos Pizza Inn was fined two hundred
and fifty thousand shillings over unsolicited marketing. So here's how to market safely. Number one: ask
first, and make it clear what you'll send. Number two: keep a record of who said yes. Number three: give an
easy way to opt out in every message. This is not legal advice. Have you ever received promo texts you never
asked for? Tell me below.
