## Narration
You'd think the biggest YouTuber in the world makes most of his money from YouTube. But in 2024, according
to Fortune, MrBeast's chocolate brand, Feastables, brought in about two hundred and fifty one million dollars
in sales, and more than twenty million dollars in profit. Meanwhile, his media business, the YouTube channel
and Beast Games, brought in similar revenue but lost nearly eighty million dollars. Why? Those giant videos
cost a fortune to make. But they do something powerful: they make millions of people want the chocolate.
Here's the lesson for your business. Number one: your content is there to earn attention. Number two: sell
something people want to buy again and again. Number three: know which part of your business actually makes
the profit. What could your business sell to the people who already watch you? Tell me below.
