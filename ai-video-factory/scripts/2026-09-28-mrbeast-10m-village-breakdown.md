## Narration
MrBeast just spent ten million dollars building an entire village in Ghana.
Here's exactly what that money bought. The village is called Abna Dakwa, in Ghana's
Eastern Region, and about one thousand and sixty two people now live there.
Number one: a school with room for more than three hundred students, plus ten homes for teachers.
Number two: a medical clinic, a clean water well, and proper toilets.
Number three: a marketplace and a community farm, so families can earn a living.
It took a year of planning and eight months of building. Now do the math.
Ten million dollars across about a thousand residents is roughly nine thousand four hundred
dollars per person. The goal: give children in cocoa-growing communities a real alternative
to illegal labour. Would you spend ten million this way? Tell me in the comments.
