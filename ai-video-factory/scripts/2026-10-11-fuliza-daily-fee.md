## Narration
Akinyi sells shoes in Kisumu. A supplier has a good deal, so she Fulizas three thousand shillings for stock.
She'll pay it back when the shoes sell. Every morning, she checks her balance, and she owes a little more.
Here's what's happening. Fuliza isn't one fee. First, a one-off access fee of one percent, plus excise duty.
Then, every night at midnight, a daily maintenance fee on whatever you still owe. For her balance, that's
thirty shillings a day, including excise. It sounds small. Fourteen days later, she has paid about four
hundred and twenty shillings in daily fees alone. That money could have bought more stock. So Akinyi changed one
habit. Every time a customer pays, she clears the Fuliza first. Small balances cost less, and paying back
fast costs the least. Rates change, so check Safaricom's current tariff. Not financial advice. Do you clear
your Fuliza fast? Tell me below.
