## Narration
Picture a clothes seller in Nairobi. Let's call him Kevin. Every sale lands in his M-Pesa. So does
money from family. Then out it goes: rent, lunch, airtime, new stock. At the end of the month, Kevin has
no idea if his business actually made a profit. Here's the fix. It's called Pochi la Biashara. It gives
you a separate business wallet on the same phone number. No new SIM. His customers go to Lipa na M-PESA,
choose Pay to Pochi la Biashara, and enter his number. The sales go into the business wallet, not his
personal one. To join, he can dial star three three four hash, choose Pochi la Biashara, accept the terms
and pick his business type. At the end of the week, Kevin can finally see what the business earned. Do you
keep business money separate? Tell me below.
