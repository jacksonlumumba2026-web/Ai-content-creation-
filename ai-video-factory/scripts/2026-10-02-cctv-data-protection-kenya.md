## Narration
Picture a hardware shop owner in Kisumu. Let's call him Otieno. After a break-in, he installs CCTV cameras.
Smart move, right? But there's something most small business owners in Kenya don't know. Under the Data
Protection Act, very small businesses are usually exempt from registering with the Data Protection
Commissioner. That's turnover under five million shillings and fewer than ten employees. But some
businesses must register no matter their size, and one of them is any business running CCTV. Others include
schools, transport operators, hospitality firms and property agents. The penalty can reach five million
shillings, or one percent of annual turnover, whichever is lower. And the regulator is active. It determined
ninety-six complaints in 2025 alone. So if you have cameras, check whether you're registered. It's cheaper
than a fine. Did you know this rule? Tell me below.
