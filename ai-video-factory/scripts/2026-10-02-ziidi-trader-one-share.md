## Narration
Picture a young mechanic in Nakuru. Let's call him Brian. He's always wanted to own a piece of a big
company. But he thinks you need a broker, piles of paperwork, and a lot of money. In February, Safaricom and
the Nairobi Securities Exchange launched Ziidi Trader. It lets you buy and sell listed shares right inside the
M-PESA app, and you can start with as little as one share. No separate broker account. No extra paperwork.
Here's how. Open the M-PESA app, choose Ziidi Trader, accept the terms, complete the verification, and enter
your PIN. Moving money between M-Pesa and Ziidi Trader is free. Two things to know. Trading fees still apply,
and orders go through during market hours, Monday to Friday. And remember, share prices can go down as well
as up. This is not financial advice. Would you buy your first share? Tell me below.
