## Narration
Picture a hardware shop owner in Nakuru. Let's call him Njoroge. It's a busy Saturday. Customers are paying,
his phone keeps buzzing, and a prompt pops up asking for his M-Pesa PIN. He's about to type it. Then he
stops, and reads it. It's not money coming in. It's money going out, to a name he doesn't know. Safaricom
has warned that fraudsters exploit people who approve prompts without checking. In their words, your M-Pesa
PIN is your final line of defense. So here are three rules. Rule one: read every prompt before your PIN. Who,
how much, and why. Rule two: if you didn't start it, cancel it. Rule three: never share your PIN with anyone.
Safaricom says it will never ask for it. Would you have caught it in time? Tell me below.
