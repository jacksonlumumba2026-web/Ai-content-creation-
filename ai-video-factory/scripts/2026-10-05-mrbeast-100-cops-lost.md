## Narration
Imagine carrying a backpack with one hundred thousand dollars in it, while one hundred police officers
hunt you. That's exactly what MrBeast and four friends did. The rules were simple. Twelve hours inside a
huge training site in Indiana. If the off-duty Indiana State Police troopers caught all five, the police
kept all five hundred thousand dollars. The team tried everything. Camouflage. Hiding spots. Even hired
lookalikes to confuse the officers. It still wasn't enough. All five were caught with about ninety minutes
left, and the police won the money. But here's the business lesson. MrBeast lost the challenge and still
won attention. The video pulled in tens of millions of views within days. Number one: a great story doesn't
need a happy ending. Number two: show the struggle, not just the win. Number three: give people someone to
root for. Would you have lasted twelve hours? Tell me below.
