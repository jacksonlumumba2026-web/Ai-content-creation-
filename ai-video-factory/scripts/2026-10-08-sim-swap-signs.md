## Narration
Otieno wakes up at two in the morning. His phone has no network. The screen says No SIM, but the SIM is
still inside. He thinks it's just Safaricom, and goes back to sleep. That's how a SIM swap often works. A
fraudster gets a new SIM card with your number, and with it, a way into your M-Pesa. It often happens at
night, while you sleep. Here are the signs. Sign one: your network disappears for no reason. Sign two: your
phone says No SIM, or emergency calls only, but you didn't touch it. So what do you do? Don't wait until
morning. Borrow a Safaricom phone, call one hundred, and ask them to block your line, so your M-Pesa can be
frozen before money moves. And never share your PIN or any code sent to you. Share this with someone who keeps
money on M-Pesa.
