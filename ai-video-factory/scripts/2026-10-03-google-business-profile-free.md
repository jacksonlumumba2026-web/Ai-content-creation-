## Narration
Picture a salon owner in Nairobi. Let's call her Njeri. Her work is great, her prices are fair, and her regulars
love her. But when someone new nearby searches 'salon near me' on Google, they see the salon across the road.
Its photos, its reviews, its phone number, its directions. Njeri doesn't show up at all. The fix is free. It's
called a Google Business Profile. And yet, industry estimates suggest around four in ten small local
businesses still have incomplete profile information. So here's the quick fix. Number one: claim and verify
your profile. Number two: add your hours, phone number, location and real photos of your work. Number three:
ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to them. People searching nearby are often ready to buy. Make sure
they find you, not the shop across the road. Is your business on Google Maps? Tell me below.
