## Narration
Picture a caterer in Eldoret. Let's call her Mercy. One day, a company calls. They want her to cater their
staff party. Then comes the question: can you send a KRA eTIMS invoice? Mercy says no, and the order goes
to someone else. Here's what she didn't know. KRA says every business, even small and informal ones, should
issue electronic tax invoices through eTIMS. And for small businesses not registered for VAT, there's a free
version called eTIMS Lite. There are three ways to use it. On the web, through eCitizen. On the eTIMS app. Or,
with no internet at all, dial star two two two hash on any phone. Mercy sends her next invoice in minutes,
and the company books her for every event after that. This is not tax advice, so check the details with KRA.
