## Narration
Brian sells shoes online in Nairobi, and a WhatsApp chatbot answers his customers day and night. Then on
Thursday, a friend sends him a headline: WhatsApp is now charging Kenyan businesses. Brian panics. Here's what
changed. From October first, businesses on the WhatsApp Business Platform, the system behind chatbots and
customer service tools, pay for service replies. About fifty-two cents per delivered message. But the first
one thousand service replies per number, every month, are free. But here's the part most people miss. If you
just use the normal WhatsApp Business app on your phone, and type to customers yourself, this charge doesn't
apply to you. So Brian does the maths. He checks how many replies his chatbot sends each month, and plans for
it. Which one do you use, the app or the platform? Tell me below.
