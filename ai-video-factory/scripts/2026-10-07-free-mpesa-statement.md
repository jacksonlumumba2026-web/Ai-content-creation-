## Narration
Picture a man in Machakos who runs a phone accessories stall. Let's call him Mutua. He applies for a loan to
buy more stock, and the lender asks for proof of his sales. Mutua has no books. He panics. Here's what he
forgot. Almost every customer pays him through M-Pesa, and his M-PESA statement shows all of it. Even
better, getting your own statement is free to request. Option one: dial star three three four hash, go to My
Account, then M-PESA Statement, choose the period, and enter your email and PIN. Option two: in the M-PESA
app, open Statements, choose the period, and tap email statement. It arrives as a password-protected PDF.
Mutua sends it with his application the same day. When did you last check your M-Pesa statement? Tell me
below.
