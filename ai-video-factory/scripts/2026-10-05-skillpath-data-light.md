## Narration
Mercy is learning web design on her phone in Kitui. Twenty minutes into a video, a message pops up. Low
balance. Her data bundle is gone, and the lesson isn't finished. Sound familiar? SkillPath Africa was made in
Kenya, for Kenyan phones. Every lesson shows its data cost before you press play, so you know if your bundle
will last. No surprises halfway through. The paths are built in levels, starting from zero, in plain
language, step by step. You can learn in English or Kiswahili. The first lesson of every path is free. After
that, a path is five hundred shillings, paid once, and it's yours for life. Mercy now checks the data cost,
buys the right bundle, and finishes every lesson. What would you learn if data wasn't a worry? The link is in
the bio.
