## Narration
Okay, so this is wild. During the Super Bowl, MrBeast walked through a vault in an ad for Salesforce and
said: solve my puzzle, and I'll give you one million dollars. According to MrBeast, around sixty million
people visited the puzzle website. A week later? Nobody had solved it. So he made it easier. Just Slack him
the secret code, and Slackbot can help with the clues. Almost a month after the game, a guy named Colin sent
the right code, and got flown out to open the vault himself. Now here's what I keep thinking about as a
business owner. Salesforce didn't just show people an ad. They gave them a game, and their product was the
tool to win it. Number one: give people something to do, not just something to watch. Number two: put your
product inside the game. Number three: give it a clear ending everyone can follow. Would you have tried to
crack it? Tell me below.
