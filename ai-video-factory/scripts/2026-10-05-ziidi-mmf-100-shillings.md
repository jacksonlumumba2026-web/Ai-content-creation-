## Narration
Picture a boda boda rider in Machakos. Let's call him Kamau. Every evening he tells himself he'll save a
little. But it never stays. The money sits in M-Pesa, and somehow it's gone by the weekend. Then he finds
Ziidi, a money market fund right inside the M-PESA app, from Safaricom and Standard Investment Bank. He can
start with as little as one hundred shillings. Here's how it works. Moving money in is free. His savings
earn interest, worked out every day. And when he needs the money, he can withdraw it back to M-Pesa for
free. Now his savings sit apart from his spending money. Two things to know. The interest rate changes over
time, and this is not a bank account, it's an investment fund licensed by the Capital Markets Authority.
This is not financial advice. How much would you put aside first? Tell me below.
