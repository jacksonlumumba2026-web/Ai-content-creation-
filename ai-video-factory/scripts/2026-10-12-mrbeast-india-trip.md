## Narration
Last week, MrBeast posted a simple caption: had an amazing trip to India. The world's biggest YouTuber, but in
India, he's still a foreigner. But look at who he was standing next to. Cricket star Hardik Pandya, copying
his famous shrug celebration on a cricket field. Indian YouTubers CarryMinati and Payal Dhare. And film star
Ranbir Kapoor, at an event for the film Ramayana in Mumbai. Here's why that matters. Their fans saw
MrBeast standing next to people they already love and trust. That trust rubs off. Number one: team up with
names people already trust. Number two: speak the local language. In India, that's cricket. Number three:
show up in person, not just online. For your business, it works the same. A new shop does better when the
local chairlady, the matatu crew, or the barber next door says it's good. Who's the person your customers
