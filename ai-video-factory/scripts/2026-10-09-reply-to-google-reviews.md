## Narration
Halima opens her phone after a long lunch rush at her restaurant in Mombasa. A new review: one star. The
food came late, the customer says, and nobody said sorry. Her first thought is to ignore it. Then she remembers
that every new customer who searches for her restaurant can read that review, and her reply. Google says
helpful replies to reviews can show that you're responsive to your customers. So she replies. Here's how to
reply well. Number one: thank the person, by name. Number two: if the review is bad, say sorry and say what
you'll do to fix it. Number three: keep it short and polite, and never share private details. The next person
who reads it doesn't just see a complaint. They see an owner who cares. Do you reply to your Google reviews?
Tell me below.
