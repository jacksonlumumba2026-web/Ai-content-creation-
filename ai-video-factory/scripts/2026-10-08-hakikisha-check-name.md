## Narration
Akinyi pays her fabric supplier by M-Pesa every Friday. This Friday, she's in a hurry. She types the number
from memory, enters the amount, and one digit is wrong. Then the screen asks her to confirm, and shows a
name she has never seen. That screen is called Hakikisha, and it's there for exactly this moment. It shows
who you're paying before the money leaves. Safaricom made it a simple yes or no, and according to TechCabal,
reversal requests dropped from about twelve thousand a day to around four thousand. Akinyi taps No. Her money
stays put. So here's the habit. Read the name. Check the amount. If anything looks wrong, tap No. It takes two
seconds. Have you ever sent money to the wrong number? Tell me below.
