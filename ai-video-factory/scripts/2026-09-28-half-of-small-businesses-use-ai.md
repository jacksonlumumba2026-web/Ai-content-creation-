## Narration
Almost half of small businesses in America already use AI. That's not hype.
It comes from the Federal Reserve's latest survey of employer firms. Forty-six percent said
they or their staff use AI, and another fifteen percent plan to start within a year.
So what are they actually using it for? Number one: writing and marketing. Social posts,
emails, product descriptions. Number two: personal productivity. Summaries, notes, and
faster research. Number three: planning and analysis. Turning messy numbers into clear
decisions. And here's the part most people miss. Most of these firms did not see their labor
costs change. What went up was productivity. So the real question isn't whether AI replaces
your team. It's whether your competitors get more done with the same team.
