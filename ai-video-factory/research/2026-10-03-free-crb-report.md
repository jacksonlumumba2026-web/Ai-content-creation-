# Research: check your CRB report free before a loan (💡 quick win; not financial advice)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Every person is entitled to one free credit report per year from each licensed CRB (e.g. TransUnion, Metropol, Creditinfo) | CIS Kenya "About CRBs"; Kenya Times explainer; UoN Finance guide |
| Request via the bureau's website/app/USSD with your national ID (e.g. Metropol *433#) | Kenya Times; pesamarket guide |
| If something is wrong you can dispute it; under the CRB Regulations 2020 the bureau must notify the lender within 5 working days | pesamarket / HelaBora (citing Reg. 50(1)) |
| Free reports can take roughly 24–72 hours to arrive | pesamarket |

Bureau names vary between sources (Experian vs TransUnion), so the script names none on screen
except as examples. Character "Wanjiru" is illustrative.
