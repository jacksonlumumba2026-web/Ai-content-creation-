# Research: WhatsApp Business Platform charges in Kenya from 1 Oct 2026 (🔥 viral, this week)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| From 1 Oct 2026, Meta charges Kenyan businesses on the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) for service replies | tech-ish (1 Oct 2026); Kenyans.co.ke; Techweez (28 Sep 2026) |
| About KSh 0.52 ($0.0040) per delivered service message | Kenyans.co.ke; Eastleigh Voice |
| First 1,000 delivered service messages per business phone number per month are free | tech-ish; Techweez |
| Marketing messages stay at about KSh 3.20–3.30 each | Eastleigh Voice / Nairobi Wire summaries |
| Businesses using only the ordinary WhatsApp Business app on a phone aren't charged under this model | Kenyans.co.ke; tech-ish |
