# Research: the "I sent you money by mistake" reversal scam (💎 hidden gem)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Official reversal: forward the original M-PESA message to 456 within 24 hours; Safaricom checks it | People Daily (Safaricom explains reversals); Plisio guide |
| Scam pattern: someone sends money (or a fake SMS), calls urgently with a "mistake" story and asks you to send it back; the original then gets reversed | paybillke M-PESA scams 2026 |
| Never send money back yourself; tell them to request a reversal through 456 | paybillke guide |
| Report fraud: call Safaricom on 100 (Safaricom line) or 234 | paybillke guide; pesatrail safety tips |
