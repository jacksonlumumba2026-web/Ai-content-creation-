# Research: Fuliza's daily maintenance fee (💎 hidden gem; not financial advice)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| One-off 1% access fee, plus 20% excise duty on the fee | People Daily (Safaricom explainer); Kenya Times |
| A daily maintenance fee is charged at midnight on the outstanding balance, plus 20% excise | People Daily |
| Balance KSh 2,501–70,000: KSh 25 daily fee + KSh 5 excise = KSh 30/day | People Daily; loanapp.co.ke 2026 rates |
| Balance KSh 0–100: no maintenance fee; 101–500: KSh 3/day total; 501–1,000: KSh 6/day total | People Daily |

Arithmetic in the script: KSh 30 × 14 days = KSh 420 (derived, not a quoted figure).
Character "Akinyi" is illustrative. Safaricom/People Daily blocked by the proxy; cross-checked via WebSearch summaries from several sources.
