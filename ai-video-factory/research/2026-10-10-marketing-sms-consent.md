# Research: marketing SMS without consent can cost you (💎 hidden gem; not legal advice)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| ODPC ordered Platinum Credit to pay KES 400,000 for unsolicited marketing texts and calls to someone who never shared data with it (Dec 2025) | Capital FM |
| Pepinos Pizza Inn was fined KES 250,000 for unsolicited marketing | Legal Express Kenya (Dec 2025); Techweez (Jan 2026) |
| Consent must be express, specific and informed; past contact isn't automatic permission; offer an opt-out | Techweez; Afriwise; BA Advocates |
| Maximum administrative fine: KES 5M or 1% of annual turnover, whichever is lower | Data Protection Act s.63 (Techweez, olmllp) |

Character "Mwangi" (salon owner sending promo texts) is illustrative.
