# Research: Pochi la Biashara — separate business money on the same number (💡 quick win)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Pochi la Biashara is an M-PESA service for small businesses to keep business money separate from personal money, on the same phone number (no new SIM) | Safaricom Pochi page/FAQ; tech-ish (Jun 2026) |
| Customers pay via Lipa na M-PESA → "Pay to Pochi La Biashara" → enter the number | Safaricom; The Star explainer (Mar 2025) |
| Register by dialling *334# → Pochi La Biashara → accept terms → business category | Safaricom; centwarrior guide |
| Owners can withdraw from Pochi at an agent directly | tech-ish; Streamlinefeed |

Not claimed: specific charges (check Safaricom rates page). Character "Kevin" is illustrative.
