# Research: register a business name on eCitizen for KES 950 (💡 quick win; not legal advice)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Business names are registered by the Business Registration Service (BRS) on eCitizen | BRS FAQ (brs.go.ke); Kenya Times guide |
| It costs KES 950 to register a business name | BRS FAQ PDF ("It costs Kshs. 950 to register a business name") |
| Steps: log in to eCitizen → BRS → Make Application → Registration of a Business Name → fill details → sign the auto-generated form and re-upload → pay the KES 950 prompt | BRS FAQ PDF |

Character "Faith" is illustrative. Not legal advice; fees can change — check eCitizen.
